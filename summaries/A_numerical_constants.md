Reference table of the numerical constants of the book, to six significant figures unless stated otherwise, all recomputed with the book's scripts: the drift log(sqrt(3)/2) and the step variance of the accelerated map, the model drift and variance per stride of the Syracuse map, total stopping time slopes in the model and observed, the central limit constant, the Chernoff rate D of the density theorem, the residue sieve exponent h, the Cesàro threshold log_2 3, the Switching MM momentum beta = log_2 3 minus 1 with its noise amplification, effective step multiplier and stability limit, and the parity autocorrelations of the model. It also gives the continued fraction of log_2 3, computed at 300 digit precision, whose large partial quotients 23 and 55 make the convergents 1054/665 and 301994/190537 exceptionally good two sided approximations; only the second lies above log_2 3 and counts for the one sided cycle bound, which jumps from 190,537 to the next rung, 10,781,274. A final table gives exact lower bounds on the number of odd elements and of halvings of a nontrivial cycle for the verified ranges 2^40, 2^60, 2^68, 2^71 and 2^72; every range up to 2^71 is established, and the 2^72 row shows what a larger range would give.
