# Chapter 8. Noise, Variance and Experiments

This chapter tests whether the parity schedule of Switching MM acts as a variance filter, using exact stationary variances on strongly convex quadratics with additive gradient noise and, for Switching MM, multiplicative noise. Every step heavy ball and Nesterov momentum are autoregressions of order two (AR(2)); on even iterates Switching MM reduces to an autoregression of order one, which gives exact variances for even, odd and phase averaged iterates. In the slow modes the noise amplification relative to gradient descent at the same step is (2+2beta+beta^2)/(2+beta), below 5/3 for every beta below one, a bound that holds on every eigendirection at step one over L, whereas every step momentum amplifies by 1 over (1 minus beta). At matched slow mode speed Switching MM has no advantage over gradient descent in the slow modes, although it is quieter in the fast ones, and the gain weight theorem shows that in the slow mode limit no stable linear periodic first order method beats gradient descent at matched speed: the noise floor is a Cauchy and Schwarz ratio of gains, equal to one only for equal gains. The chapter also gives the Pareto frontier between rate and noise for period two step ratios, ending at the Chebyshev schedule, and a mean square stability threshold under multiplicative noise. Experiments on quadratics, logistic regression and minibatch gradients, against heavy ball, Nesterov and Adam, are reconciled with the theory. Section 8.9 goes beyond quadratics: over strongly convex smooth functions the stationary error of Switching MM stays below five thirds of that of gradient descent for small fluctuations, and in the limit of small steps at fixed temperature the stationary laws converge to Gibbs laws at temperature V(beta) T and T (the temperature law), with ratio at most V(beta) cubed in one dimension and a ten ninths cap on the penalty of averaging; Problem S5 asks for the supremum of the ratio.

## From the literature

Stationary variance of an autoregression of order two through the Yule and Walker equations (Brockwell and Davis 1991); heavy ball on quadratics (Polyak 1987); equivalence of momentum SGD and SGD with a rescaled step at small step sizes (Yuan, Ying and Sayed 2016); heavy ball and Nesterov need not improve on SGD for least squares (Kidambi, Netrapalli, Jain and Kakade 2018); the period two Chebyshev schedule (Young 1953; Golub and Varga 1961); Adam (Kingma and Ba 2015); the Breast Cancer Wisconsin data (Wolberg, Street and Mangasarian 1995).

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Exact stationary variance of Switching MM under additive gradient noise](../RESULTS.md#smm_stationary_variance_additive_noise) (Theorem 8.4, page 126; proved): An exact formula for the stationary variance of Switching MM on a quadratic with additive gradient noise, for even iterates, odd iterates and their phase average, as a rational function of the normalized step and the momentum coefficient. New in this book.
- [Slow mode noise amplification of Switching MM](../RESULTS.md#smm_slow_mode_noise_amplification) (Corollary 8.5, page 127; proved): In the slow modes Switching MM amplifies gradient noise, relative to gradient descent at the same step, by an explicit function of the momentum coefficient that equals 1.359 at the Collatz value. New in this book.
- [Noise bound for Switching MM uniform in the normalized step](../RESULTS.md#smm_variance_bound_uniform_in_normalized_step) (Proposition 8.7, page 128; proved): On a quadratic with additive gradient noise, on every eigendirection with normalized step up to one, the stationary variance of Switching MM lies between that of gradient descent and 5/3 times it, uniformly over momentum coefficients below one, whereas every step momentum amplifies slow mode noise by a factor that grows without bound as the coefficient tends to one. New in this book.
- [Noise floor at matched speed for momentum methods and Switching MM](../RESULTS.md#noise_floor_at_matched_speed) (Proposition 8.9, page 130; proved): At matched slow mode speed Switching MM pays a small noise floor penalty over gradient descent, 1.051 at the Collatz momentum and at most 10/9; it is the period two analogue of the result of Yuan, Ying and Sayed (2016) that every step heavy ball and Nesterov match gradient descent exactly. New in this book.
- [Gain weight theorem for the noise floor of periodic first order methods](../RESULTS.md#gain_weight_theorem) (Theorem 8.12, page 133; proved): For any linear periodic first order method on a quadratic that is stable at zero normalized step and has positive total gain, the slow mode noise floor at matched speed relative to gradient descent is a Cauchy and Schwarz ratio of the step gains, at least one, and equal to one only when all gains are equal. New in this book.
- [Rate and noise Pareto frontier for period two step schedules](../RESULTS.md#rate_and_noise_for_period_two_steps) (Proposition 8.14, page 136; proved): For period two cyclic gradient steps, the worst case contraction and the noise floor at matched speed depend on the step ratio through one number, which gives the exact Pareto frontier between rate and noise, ending at the Chebyshev schedule. New in this book.
- [Switching MM under multiplicative gradient noise](../RESULTS.md#smm_multiplicative_noise) (Proposition 8.15, page 139; proved): Under multiplicative gradient noise Switching MM has a closed form stationary second moment, an unchanged slow mode ratio to gradient descent under least squares scaling, and a mean square stability threshold given by the gains under diffusive scaling. New in this book.
- [Worst case stationary error of Switching MM over the class of strongly convex smooth functions](../RESULTS.md#worst_case_noise_floor_of_smm_over_the_class) (Theorem 8.18, page 141; proved): Stationary mean square errors of Switching MM on the class of mu strongly convex L smooth functions are bounded by closed forms; when (1+beta) alpha (L+mu) is at most 2 the bounds are attained by the quadratic, and the worst case is below five thirds of that of gradient descent. New in this book.
- [Five thirds for small fluctuations](../RESULTS.md#five_thirds_for_small_fluctuations) (Theorem 8.19, page 142; proved): For mu strongly convex L smooth functions with Lipschitz Hessian, and i.i.d. shocks with bounded fourth moment, the stationary covariances of Switching MM and gradient descent are within a first order error of the quadratic formulas, so their ratio is below five thirds when the fluctuations are small. New in this book.
- [The temperature law: Gibbs limit of Switching MM at small steps](../RESULTS.md#temperature_law_of_switching_mm) (Theorem 8.20, page 144; proved): For mu strongly convex L smooth f and i.i.d. shocks with finite fourth moment, at fixed temperature the stationary laws of Switching MM converge, in the limit of small steps, to the Gibbs law at temperature V(beta) T, and those of gradient descent to that at T, so the error ratio converges to an explicit ratio of Gibbs second moments. New in this book.
- [Bounds on the temperature law in one dimension](../RESULTS.md#bounds_in_one_dimension_on_the_temperature_law) (Proposition 8.21, page 145; proved): In one dimension, for convex f, the second moment of the Gibbs law satisfies m(sT) at most s^3 m(T) for every s at least 1, and at most s^2 m(T) for symmetric f, so the error ratio is at most V(beta)^3, and at most V(beta)^2 for symmetric f. New in this book.
- [Ten ninths under averaging](../RESULTS.md#ten_ninths_under_averaging) (Proposition 8.23, page 147; proved): The asymptotic variance of the time average of Switching MM on a quadratic eigendirection exceeds that of gradient descent by the factor W(a,beta), which lies in [1, 10/9). New in this book.
- [Problem S5: Beyond the temperature law](../RESULTS.md#beyond_the_temperature_law_problem) (Problem S5, page 148; open): Find the supremum over the class of the ratio of stationary mean square errors of Switching MM to gradient descent, in the diffusion limit and at the step 1/L, and extend the one dimensional bounds to several dimensions. New in this book.
