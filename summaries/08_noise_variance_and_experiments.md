# Chapter 8. Noise, Variance and Experiments

This chapter tests whether the parity schedule of Switching MM acts as a variance filter, using exact stationary variances on strongly convex quadratics with additive gradient noise and, for Switching MM, multiplicative noise. Every step heavy ball and Nesterov momentum are autoregressions of order two (AR(2)); on even iterates Switching MM reduces to an autoregression of order one, which gives exact variances for even, odd and phase averaged iterates. In the slow modes the noise amplification relative to gradient descent at the same step is (2+2beta+beta^2)/(2+beta), below 5/3 for every beta below one, a bound that holds on every eigendirection at step one over L, whereas every step momentum amplifies by 1 over (1 minus beta). At matched slow mode speed Switching MM has no advantage over gradient descent in the slow modes, although it is quieter in the fast ones, and the gain weight theorem shows that in the slow mode limit no stable linear periodic first order method beats gradient descent at matched speed: the noise floor is a Cauchy and Schwarz ratio of gains, equal to one only for equal gains. The chapter also gives the Pareto frontier between rate and noise for period two step ratios, ending at the Chebyshev schedule, and a mean square stability threshold under multiplicative noise. Experiments on quadratics, logistic regression and minibatch gradients, against heavy ball, Nesterov and Adam, are reconciled with the theory.

## From the literature

Stationary variance of an autoregression of order two through the Yule and Walker equations (Brockwell and Davis 1991); heavy ball on quadratics (Polyak 1987); equivalence of momentum SGD and SGD with a rescaled step at small step sizes (Yuan, Ying and Sayed 2016); heavy ball and Nesterov need not improve on SGD for least squares (Kidambi, Netrapalli, Jain and Kakade 2018); the period two Chebyshev schedule (Young 1953; Golub and Varga 1961); Adam (Kingma and Ba 2015); the Breast Cancer Wisconsin data (Wolberg, Street and Mangasarian 1995).

## New results

<!-- results:begin -->
- [Exact stationary variance of Switching MM under additive gradient noise](../RESULTS.md#smm_stationary_variance_additive_noise) (Theorem 8.4, page 128; proved): An exact formula for the stationary variance of Switching MM on a quadratic with additive gradient noise, for even iterates, odd iterates and their phase average, as a rational function of the normalized step and the momentum coefficient. New in this book.
- [Slow mode noise amplification of Switching MM](../RESULTS.md#smm_slow_mode_noise_amplification) (Corollary 8.5, page 129; proved): In the slow modes Switching MM amplifies gradient noise, relative to gradient descent at the same step, by an explicit function of the momentum coefficient that equals 1.359 at the Collatz value. New in this book.
- [Noise bound for Switching MM uniform in the normalized step](../RESULTS.md#smm_variance_bound_uniform_in_normalized_step) (Proposition 8.7, page 130; proved): On a quadratic with additive gradient noise, on every eigendirection with normalized step up to one, the stationary variance of Switching MM lies between that of gradient descent and 5/3 times it, uniformly over momentum coefficients below one, whereas every step momentum amplifies slow mode noise by a factor that grows without bound as the coefficient tends to one. New in this book.
- [Noise floor at matched speed for momentum methods and Switching MM](../RESULTS.md#noise_floor_at_matched_speed) (Proposition 8.9, page 132; proved): At matched slow mode speed Switching MM pays a small noise floor penalty over gradient descent, 1.051 at the Collatz momentum and at most 10/9; it is the period two analogue of the result of Yuan, Ying and Sayed (2016) that every step heavy ball and Nesterov match gradient descent exactly. New in this book.
- [Gain weight theorem for the noise floor of periodic first order methods](../RESULTS.md#gain_weight_theorem) (Theorem 8.12, page 135; proved): For any linear periodic first order method on a quadratic that is stable at zero normalized step and has positive total gain, the slow mode noise floor at matched speed relative to gradient descent is a Cauchy and Schwarz ratio of the step gains, at least one, and equal to one only when all gains are equal. New in this book.
- [Rate and noise Pareto frontier for period two step schedules](../RESULTS.md#rate_and_noise_for_period_two_steps) (Proposition 8.14, page 137; proved): For period two cyclic gradient steps, the worst case contraction and the noise floor at matched speed depend on the step ratio through one number, which gives the exact Pareto frontier between rate and noise, ending at the Chebyshev schedule. New in this book.
- [Switching MM under multiplicative gradient noise](../RESULTS.md#smm_multiplicative_noise) (Proposition 8.15, page 141; proved): Under multiplicative gradient noise Switching MM has a closed form stationary second moment, an unchanged slow mode ratio to gradient descent under least squares scaling, and a mean square stability threshold given by the gains under diffusive scaling. New in this book.
